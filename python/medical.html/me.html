Our Story

Welcome to Medicare Plus, a trusted name in healthcare located in the heart of Najibabad, Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh – 246763. Since the very beginning, our vision has been simple yet powerful: to bring health, hope, and happiness to every family we serve.

Healthcare is not just about medicines—it is about care, compassion, and commitment. At Medicare Plus, we believe that behind every prescription there is a story, behind every medicine there is a family, and behind every patient there is a dream to live a healthy and fulfilled life.

Our journey started with one small dream: to make quality medicines, healthcare products, and medical guidance accessible to everyone in our community. Over the years, we have built trust through honesty, affordability, and a genuine dedication to serve. Every customer who walks through our doors is not just a buyer—they become part of the Medicare Plus family.

We understand that health is life's greatest blessing. That's why we go beyond simply selling medicines. We educate, we guide, and we ensure that every person receives the right support and care they truly deserve.

Our Mission

Our mission at Medicare Plus is to provide reliable, affordable, and high-quality healthcare solutions that improve the quality of life for our community. We are committed to:

  • • Offering genuine and authentic medicines at fair prices
  • • Providing excellent customer service with care and compassion
  • • Creating awareness about health and well-being in society
  • • Supporting patients with knowledge and guidance
  • • Building long-term trust and relationships with families

Our Vision

Our vision is to make Medicare Plus a symbol of trust and hope in healthcare. We dream of a future where every person, regardless of background, can access essential healthcare products and live a healthier, happier life.

We aim to become more than just a medical store—we want to be a health partner that people rely on in their journey toward wellness. By embracing technology, modern healthcare practices, and compassionate service, Medicare Plus aspires to stand as a shining example of care in our community.
